A little about me

Hi, I'm Alpana!

I’m a data and AI-focused technologist who designs intelligent systems with scale, reliability, and responsibility in mind. I work at the intersection of analytics, engineering, and decision science, with a strong emphasis on building solutions that are transparent, explainable, and safe to deploy in complex, real-world environments.

I currently work as a Senior Business Analyst at Delta Air Lines, where I operate on large, high-impact datasets and partner closely with engineering, product, and leadership teams. My work involves defining metrics, building analytical frameworks, and delivering insights that support operational stability, customer experience, and strategic decisions at scale.

My approach goes beyond dashboards and models. I focus on end-to-end problem ownership, from data quality and pipeline design to evaluation, monitoring, and communication of results. I am particularly passionate about responsible and ethical AI, and I believe strong governance, clear metrics, and explainability are essential for deploying AI systems in regulated and high-stakes domains.

I bring hands-on experience with SQL, Python, Power BI, and AWS, along with a solid engineering foundation in Java, C++, HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript, C#, and ASP.NET. This hybrid background allows me to collaborate effectively with ML engineers, data engineers, and product teams while maintaining strong business context and execution focus.

I hold dual master’s degrees in Information Systems and Data Analytics, and I am a Certified Scrum Master with strong project management and delivery skills. I am comfortable leading complex initiatives, navigating ambiguity, and aligning technical work with business and compliance requirements.

What teams value most in my work is judgment. I ask the right questions early, design with long-term impact in mind, and communicate trade-offs clearly. I bring discipline, curiosity, and creativity to problem-solving, supported by my interests in painting, cooking, and baking, which help me stay thoughtful and balanced in high-pressure environments.

With over six years of experience across analytics, software, and data-driven systems, I am well-suited for AI Engineer or Principal Analyst roles where trust, scale, and execution matter as much as innovation.

I also share my ongoing learning and perspectives on data, AI, and responsible technology on my blog: The Intellectual Talks
