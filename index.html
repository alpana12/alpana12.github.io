About Me

Hi, I'm Alpana!

With over 10 years bridging software engineering, data science, system design, and AI, I bring a rare hybrid perspective that helps me translate complex technical work into actionable business impact. What drives my work is a deep commitment to ethical and responsible AI. I believe technology should serve people thoughtfully, which is why I volunteer at AI conferences, speak at hackathons, and judge innovation competitions whenever I can. I'm drawn to companies that genuinely value diversity, inclusivity, and making a meaningful difference in the world.

My journey from India to the United States taught me so much about myself and the world around me. That experience shaped my desire to create positive impact and share knowledge with others. When I'm not diving into data or building dashboards, you'll find me spending time with my rescue dog, painting, cooking, or writing technical posts for my blog, The Intellectual Talks (where several drafts are patiently waiting to be published!).

I hold dual master's degrees in Information Systems and Data Analytics, and I'm a Certified Scrum Master with deep expertise in SQL, Python, Power BI, and AWS. What teams value most is my judgment: I ask the right questions early, design with long-term impact in mind, and communicate trade-offs clearly—whether I'm working with executives, engineers, or product teams.

I'm seeking senior, impactful roles where trust, scale, and execution matter as much as innovation. If you're building responsible AI systems or data products that solve real problems, I'd love to connect.
