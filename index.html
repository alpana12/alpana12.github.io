A little about me

Hi, I'm Alpana!

I’m a data-driven technologist with a strong foundation in software development and a deep passion for responsible, ethical AI. I work at the intersection of data, analytics, and engineering—using technology not just to build systems, but to build trustworthy ones.

My technical toolkit spans SQL, Python, Power BI, AWS, and modern analytics platforms, along with hands-on experience in Java, C++, HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript, C#, and ASP.NET. As a data analyst, I enjoy transforming messy, complex data into clear insights that support better decisions and measurable impact.

I’m especially interested in how AI and analytics can be designed with transparency, fairness, and accountability in mind—whether that’s through clean data pipelines, explainable metrics, or governance-aware dashboards. For me, ethical AI isn’t an afterthought; it’s part of good system design.

Outside of work, I love reading (mostly fiction, with the occasional biography or philosophy), exploring new places, and expressing creativity through origami and painting. I strongly believe creativity fuels better problem-solving, sharper analysis, and a more thoughtful approach to technology.

I’m a Computer Science graduate from Northeastern University, Boston, with over six years of experience building and analyzing systems. I currently work as a Senior Associate Software Engineer / Data Analyst, where I focus on data quality, analytics, and scalable solutions.

I share things I’m learning—across data, AI, and technology—on my blog: The Intellectual Talks
